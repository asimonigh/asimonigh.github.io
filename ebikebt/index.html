How it works

Connect in seconds

No cables, no setup headaches. Three steps and you're riding with live data.

Power on your bike

Wake your e-bike's display or motor system, just like you would before a ride.

Open & tap Connect

eBike Sync discovers your bike over Bluetooth Low Energy in one tap.

Ride with live data

Your dashboard comes alive: speed, battery, range, assist mode and more.
