# Support — eBike Sync

Need help with eBike Sync? We're happy to help.

## Contact

**Email:** [ebikesyncapp@gmail.com](mailto:ebikesyncapp@gmail.com)

We usually reply within 2–3 business days.

When reporting a connection problem, the fastest way to help us is to attach a
diagnostic log: in the app, go to **Settings → Send logs to support**. This opens
your email app with the log attached so you can review and send it.

## Supported e-bikes

eBike Sync connects over Bluetooth to:

- A growing range of e-bike Bluetooth displays and motor systems
- Compatible cycle computers

Don't see your bike? Email us — we're actively expanding compatibility.

## Frequently asked questions

### The app can't find my bike
- Make sure your bike / display is powered on and awake.
- Enable Bluetooth on your phone and grant the app the Bluetooth permission when prompted.
- Stay within a few meters of the bike during the scan.
- If it still doesn't appear, fully close and reopen the app, then try again.

### It connects but I see no data
Power-cycle the bike's display and reconnect. If it persists, send us a
diagnostic log (see Contact above).

### Managing your subscription
eBike Sync Premium is an auto-renewable annual subscription billed by Apple.
- **Manage or cancel:** iPhone **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**.
- **Restore a purchase** (e.g. after reinstalling): open the app and tap **Restore Purchases**.

### Privacy
Your bike data stays on your device. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for details.
